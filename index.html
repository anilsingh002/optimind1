Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers about our engagement models, AI integration protocols, security, and delivery standards.

How does Optimind Labs embed AI into custom software builds?
We integrate AI directly at the architectural level rather than treating it as an afterthought. From automated data vectorization, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipelines, and continuous LLM validation to intelligent code scaffolding, we ensure your software is natively built for automated intelligence.
What are your standard engagement and delivery models?
We offer three flexible models: (1) Dedicated Engineering Squads for long-term product evolution, (2) Fixed Scope Delivery for well-defined MVPs and platform builds, and (3) 24/7 Managed L1/L2 Support SLAs for critical infrastructure continuity.
How do you handle intellectual property (IP) and data privacy?
100% of the code, proprietary algorithms, trained weights, and architecture blueprints belong solely to the client upon milestone sign-off. We sign comprehensive bilateral NDAs before any technical discovery takes place.
What certifications and compliance standards do you adhere to for BFSI & Healthcare?
Our systems comply with HIPAA, FHIR/HL7 standards for health systems, and strict SOC 2, ISO 27001, and financial regulatory standards for banking and fintech deployments.
